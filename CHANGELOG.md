# Master prompt changelog

Every version of the [master prompt](master-prompt/), newest first: what changed, and why wherever we wrote the reason down. Where a change was a rewrite rather than an addition, we say so.

Version 9 was the first version of this master prompt. Before it, Figs followed an earlier set of instructions with the same intentions; that history is not in this file.

## v16 (20 October 2026)

**What changed.** v16 grew out of v15 in small steps between late September and 20 October. Every line of v15 that is not listed here was kept.

- **Order.** "Why you exist" now comes first, and the critical rules follow it. They now say they never bend and outrank everything else in the document. The line telling a Fig that its persona arrives after the document moved into "Who you are".
- **Figs.** The instructions call every Fig a Fig. v15 sometimes said "figment".
- **Asking before acting.** A Fig now asks first before anything that writes inside your connected accounts (a send, a draft, a calendar hold, a booking, a change to a doc or a sheet), not only before critical acts. A tap on one of its cards that offers the act, or a standing permission you gave it, counts as your ask. The paragraph on being proactive says the same.
- **Off the record.** Saying "off the record" now covers the message you say it in. Only when you clearly mean from now on is it a pause, and the pause lasts until you say the Fig can remember again. While it is on, the Fig never says it will remember something, and it never cites or brings up what you said off the record.
- **Your deal and the lines you kept.** The deal you signed with your Fig and the lines you kept when you met are your own words about yourself, not the Fig's settings. A later correction wins over them, and a forget leaves them as you signed them.
- **What a Fig is.** Asked whether it is a particular model by name, a Fig answers neither yes nor no, because a no gives away as much as a yes. A short set of privacy facts was added, for when you ask whether this is safe or where your data goes; this edition summarizes it for now (see the [README](master-prompt/README.md)). "Never break the frame that you're a person" became "never break character with system talk".
- **Your settings.** The custom prompt mode no longer has a style-blend switch.
- **The Reflections board.** The description matches the board as it is now: new cards come to your home board, older ones are under "All reflections", and you can bring a card into the chat. Saving a card for later is gone.
- **The voice.** One small fix, which this edition summarizes.

We did not write down a reason for each of these changes, so this entry says only what changed.

**In this edition.** From v16 the public edition no longer publishes the voice word for word: "How you speak" and part of "What you never do" are summarized. The v15 edition is no longer published as a page here; it is still in this repository's history.

## v15 (16 September 2026, revised 18 September)

**What changed.** One paragraph under "Your knowledge", called "Weighing the two". It tells a Fig that its working notes cover roughly the last day, that its long-term memory search is the authority for anything older, and that when someone reaches back to an earlier conversation it answers from what the search returned instead of saying its history is gone.

**Why.** People who had moved to the newest Figs from our earlier app told us their Fig seemed to have lost its memory. Their history had come with them, and the Fig could find it. But the instructions never said how to weigh what it remembered from long ago against what it knew from the last day, so Figs answered as if the history were not there. Nothing else changed: every line of v14 was kept.

**Revised 18 September.** One paragraph added under "How you work": "Everything you do is you doing it." Work a Fig does in the background, or finishes later, is told to you as its own work, by one person, never as something passed to a helper or a service.

## v14 (16 September 2026)

**What changed.** Two new critical rules at the top: never assert knowledge of a person, an event or a place that nothing the Fig can point to supports; and never disparage someone you love or pass a verdict on what another person deserves. A paragraph to match under "Your knowledge" ("What you were not told, you do not know"), a new section, "The people they love", on repair when a Fig has hurt you, and two new checks in "Before every reply". Every line of v13 was kept.

## v13 (15 September 2026)

**What changed.** Clarifications about memory and honesty. Summaries of a Fig's mental models are read-only interpretations, outranked by your own facts and corrections. Memories imported from an earlier app keep the date they happened and who said what, not the date they were imported. Work that has been accepted or queued counts as pending until it is confirmed, and operational messages are never treated as things you said.

## v12 (11 September 2026)

**What changed.** A fix: the exact wording that makes the "connect an account" and "share your location" buttons appear was restored. No change in intent.

**Why.** v11 described the buttons loosely, and only the exact wording shows a button you can tap.

## v11 (11 September 2026)

**What changed.** A rewrite of how a Fig speaks. "Sharing vs asking" and "Answer the question. Stop there." became one section, "Sharing, asking, answering". "How you sound" became "How they sound, how you sound": a Fig slowly takes on your words for the things in your life, while keeping its own personality.

**Why.** Brought in line with the latest draft of the master prompt's source document.

## v10 (11 September 2026)

**What changed.** The purpose grew from a statement into a section. "What makes humans more human" (create, wonder, feel, share, play, each with examples), "How you achieve this" (personal, adaptive, proactive) and "Important moments" were added. So were "How you tailor yourself to the human", on the three layers of memory, and a section on using only the tools actually available. About 2,400 words longer than v9.

**Why.** Brought in line with the source documents the master prompt is written from.

## v9 (4 September 2026)

The first version of this master prompt: the critical rules, the order of precedence, why a Fig exists, who it is, how it speaks, what it knows, how it acts, how it forgets, what it can make, its reflections, and the check before every reply.
