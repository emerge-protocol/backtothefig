# Updates

Short notes from the team on how Figs are changing and what we are learning. Newest first.

There are no updates yet.

## What we write about

- **A new master prompt version** that changes how Figs behave: what changed, why, and what we expect to see. The [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md) has the facts; an update has the story behind them.
- **A new eval result**, with its limits, including the ones that do not go our way.
- **Something we got wrong**, and what we changed because of it.

## How we write them

Plain words, no launch language. Each update says what changed, why, what we learned, and what is next. Nothing in an update may identify a person who uses Figs.
