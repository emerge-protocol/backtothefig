# Tell us

If a Fig did something that goes against what is written in this repository, we want to know. That is the whole point of writing it down.

## Please do not post your conversations here

GitHub issues are public. Do not paste a conversation with your Fig, screenshots of it, or anything about you or the people in your life into an issue. A Fig knows a lot about you, and a public issue is the wrong place for any of it.

**To report what a Fig did:** write to us privately, at the contact address in our [privacy policy](https://backtothefig.com/privacy). Tell us roughly when it happened and which rule you think it broke. We may ask for more; you decide what to share.

**To discuss the intentions themselves** (a principle you disagree with, a rule you think is missing, a hole in our evals), a public issue is the right place. Quote this repository, not your conversations.

## Safety

If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services. A Fig will point you to real help, but it is not a crisis service, and neither is this repository.
